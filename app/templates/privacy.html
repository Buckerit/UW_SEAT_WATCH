Privacy

UW Seat Watch collects the email address you provide and the course section you choose to watch.

That information is used to verify your email address, send requested seat alerts, and manage unsubscribe requests.

Email delivery is handled through Brevo. UW Seat Watch does not sell your email address or use it for newsletters.

You can unsubscribe from a watch using the unsubscribe link in any watch email. For privacy questions, email {{ contact_email }}.
